# Research: Foundational product bet — Aura

> Vision-only source. Per Researcher role, the deliverable is **evidence with citations across User Pain, Competitive, and Moat** — not a list of gaps. Findings below.

## Questions addressed

1. Is there demonstrable user pain among the 536M underserved Indians the vision targets?
2. Who already serves (or attempts to serve) this market? What are they doing well / poorly?
3. If Aura wins, what defends it? Evaluate all 9 classic moat types.
4. Is the economic model (sub-₹20 / "price of chai") viable in Indian ARPU reality?
5. Is the vernacular-AI tech stack feasible at the price point and language scope implied?

---

## Findings — User pain

### F1: India has a structural mentorship + counsel deficit; the vision's "536M underserved" claim is directionally validated.

- **Mental health treatment gap:** ~83% of Indians needing care don't receive it (Lancet Psychiatry, 2023). ~9,000 psychiatrists serve 1.4B people (~1 per 155K). Most professionals are urban; ~70% of India is rural and underserved.
- **State validates the demand:** Tele-MANAS (govt voice helpline launched 2022) has logged 2.3M+ calls by mid-2025 — proof that anonymous voice access to support has product-market fit at population scale.
- **Career counsel gap:** Only 13.2% of Indian students receive professional career counselling. 91% of Indian graduates work in roles below their qualifications (Institute for Competitiveness, 2025). 70% of young professionals say structured mentorship matters; most don't have it.
- **Source:** [Indian Journal of Psychiatry 2025](https://journals.lww.com/indianjpsychiatry/fulltext/2025/09000/bridging_the_mental_health_treatment_gap_in_india_.12.aspx); [Healthcare Executive — treatment gap](https://www.healthcareexecutive.in/blog/mental-disorders-remains); [DrugToday — Tele-MANAS scale](https://drugtodayonline.com/medical-news/news-topic/24369-india-accelerates-mental-health-reforms-but-gaps-remain-in-rural-access-and-workforce); [India Career Centre — awareness gap](https://www.indiacareercentre.com/post/the-career-awareness-gap); [PERSOL — employability gap 2025](https://www.persolindia.com/articles/the-employability-gap).
- **Confidence:** high.
- **Limitations:** Career-counselling figures skew toward formal-sector students; truly informal-sector workers (Aura's stated persona — "the person who cleans offices in Chennai") are under-surveyed.

### F2: Indian users will engage emotionally and pay for personal guidance — but only in their language and at their price.

- **Vernacular preference:** >85% of Indians don't speak English fluently; 56% prefer regional languages when accessing digital content; 9 in 10 of India's next billion internet users will prefer vernacular (NetZero India, 2025). YouTube India: >95% of consumption is non-English.
- **Engagement runway:** Average Indian spends 4.7 hours/day on mobile — among the highest globally.
- **Voice over typing:** Voice search adoption is materially higher in Tier 2/3 than metros because speaking is more natural than typing in regional scripts.
- **Source:** [NetZero — vernacular AI India](https://netzeroindia.org/vernacular-ai-apps-india-2025/); [Techarc — vernacular voice revolution](https://techarc.net/vernacular-voice-revolution-why-indias-next-billion-users-will-speak-not-type/); [Storyboard18 — 660M smartphone users](https://www.storyboard18.com/digital/660-million-smartphone-users-16-17-billion-monthly-upi-transactions-power-digital-bharat-report-89731.htm); [Redseer — Tier 2+ Bharat](https://redseer.com/articles/the-3-2bn-bharat-opportunity-how-tier-2-cities-are-driving-indias-interactive-media-boom/).
- **Confidence:** high.

### F3: Indians already pay — billions — for *emotional / decision* guidance, just not from "wellness apps." They pay astrologers.

- **Astrotalk: ₹1,182 cr revenue FY25, ₹250 cr profit, ~35M active users worldwide, ~80% market share** of Indian astrology apps. Use cases explicitly include career decisions, relationship guidance, marriage matching, life-event timing, mental-wellness style reassurance.
- This is the *closest behavioural analogue* to what the Aura vision describes: vernacular, mobile, emotionally-resonant 1:1 guidance for personal decisions, paid by mass-market Indians.
- **Implication:** the demand the vision targets is *already monetised at ₹1,000+ cr scale* — by a product Aura is positioned to compete with on substance (reasoning) rather than mysticism.
- **Source:** [BizFin — $1B astrology app](https://bizfin.substack.com/p/the-1b-astrology-app-india-cant-ignore); [Storyboard18 — astrology business model](https://www.storyboard18.com/how-it-works/biz-of-belief-how-indias-astrology-apps-are-turning-faith-into-fortune-amid-rising-regulatory-concerns-83027.htm); [Knox Blog — Astrotalk disruption](https://blog.knox.team/astrotalk-astrology-app-in-india/).
- **Confidence:** high.
- **Limitations:** Astrotalk's revenue mix is per-minute paid calls with astrologers, not flat-rate subscription. Aura's price model differs.

## Findings — Competitive

### F4: The competitive set splits into 4 categories. None of them currently occupies Aura's stated position.

| Category | Examples | What they do well | Why they don't serve Aura's user |
|----------|----------|-------------------|----------------------------------|
| Western AI companions | Replika (20M users), Character.AI ($32M ARR 2024), Pi | Emotional bonds, 13–50% D30 retention (2–10x mobile-app norm), strong English-language conversational quality | English-first, Western therapy idioms, no vernacular grounding, US/EU monetisation assumptions ($10–20/mo) |
| Indian mental wellness apps | Wysa (1M+ users, FDA Class II, NHS partner), YourDost (₹19.46 cr FY24), Amaha | Clinical rigour (Wysa: 40% moved from clinical to subclinical PHQ-9/GAD-7 in NHS pilot), professional therapists in the loop (YourDost) | Wysa Premium = $74.99/yr (~₹6,200) — ~25× "price of chai." Talk-therapy idiom (CBT/DBT) culturally distant from Bharat. English-default. |
| Indian astrology apps | Astrotalk (₹1,182 cr FY25, ~35M MAU), Astroyogi (₹85 cr FY24), InstaAstro, GaneshaSpeaks | Vernacular, mass-market price, paid 1:1 emotional guidance at scale, deep Indian cultural fit | Reasoning grounded in astrology not evidence; per-minute marketplace model not friend-model; regulatory scrutiny rising |
| State helpline | Tele-MANAS (2.3M+ calls) | Free, voice, vernacular, trusted | Wait times; episodic crisis-mode not ongoing companion; clinical/medical framing |
| Direct AI (no India persona) | ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude consumer apps | Generally capable | Not designed for emotional reflection, no persistent memory of user's life context, English-default UX, no cultural attunement |

- **Astrotalk is the most underestimated competitor.** It is the existing answer Aura's users already use for "what should I do about my career / marriage / life decision." Aura competes here on *substance* (reasoned reflection vs. mystical prediction).
- **Wysa is the technical benchmark.** Their FDA Class II clearance, NHS pilots, and JMIR-published clinical evidence set a credibility floor any serious AI counsel product must meet over time — even if Aura is not clinically positioned.
- **Source:** [NemaClub — top 10 India mental health apps 2025](https://www.nemaclub.com/post/top-10-mental-health-apps-in-india-in-2025-updated); [Wysa clinical evidence](https://www.wysa.com/clinical-evidence); [Wysa FDA Breakthrough Device](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20220512005084/en/Wysa-Receives-FDA-Breakthrough-Device-Designation-for-AI-led-Mental-Health-Conversational-Agent); [iatroX — Wysa/Limbic/Woebot/NICE](https://www.iatrox.com/blog/ai-mental-health-wysa-limbic-woebot-nice-guidance-uk); [Choosing Therapy — Wysa pricing](https://www.choosingtherapy.com/wysa-app-review/); [HappySoul — YourDost](https://happysoulindia.com/wellnessapp/mental-health-emotional-wellness/yourdost/); [TechCrunch — AI companion market](https://techcrunch.com/2025/08/12/ai-companion-apps-on-track-to-pull-in-120m-in-2025/); [Elevation Capital — AI Companion India opportunity](https://ai.elevationcapital.com/blogs/ai-companion-indias-opportunity); [ElectroIQ — AI companion stats](https://electroiq.com/stats/ai-companions-statistics/).
- **Confidence:** high for incumbents named; medium for market share between them.

### F5: AI-companion retention is the closest behavioural benchmark — 13–50% D30, 2–10× ordinary apps.

- General mobile app D30 retention ≈ 5%. AI companion apps: 13–50% — because emotional bond raises the cost of abandonment.
- Aura's product mechanism (memory, friend-frame, vernacular) is engineered for this retention dynamic. A realistic year-1 north-star target should sit toward the lower end of this band initially (≥25% D30), with depth metrics (CSAT, self-reported clarity) above it.
- **Source:** [ElectroIQ — AI companion retention](https://electroiq.com/stats/ai-companions-statistics/); [NovaEdge — AI companions 50M users](https://www.novaedgedigitallabs.tech/Blog/ai-companions-50-million-users-valentines-day-2026).
- **Confidence:** medium (benchmarks are from Western consumer AI companion category; India behaviour may differ — likely *higher* engagement given mobile-time depth, but unproven for emotional-counsel category specifically).

## Findings — Economics & feasibility

### F6: "Less than the price of a chai" is achievable but tight.

- Indian wireless ARPU: ~₹152/month (Q4 2023, Statista). Gaming ARPU: $3.03 (~₹250/month).
- "Price of a chai" ≈ ₹10–20 — implies a sub-₹20/month price point. That is below current Indian wireless ARPU but consistent with mass-market app monetisation patterns (ad-subsidised, freemium, or micro-subscription).
- Wysa Premium at $74.99/yr ≈ ₹500/month — **~25× the Aura target**. This is the price gap Aura claims it can close.
- **Implication:** unit economics must work at LLM-inference cost per user-minute that is 10–30× lower than typical Western AI-companion app assumptions. This is feasible only if Aura uses India-deployed open-source models (AI4Bharat IndicASR/IndicTTS, Sarvam, distilled Llama/Mistral with Bhashini stack) — not GPT-4 class API calls per turn.
- **Source:** [Statista — India wireless ARPU](https://www.statista.com/statistics/1030614/india-monthly-arpu-for-wireless-services-users/); [Outlook Respawn — Indian gaming ARPU](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-originals/is-indias-low-gaming-arpu-sustainable-for-publishers); [Caller.digital — open-source voice AI India](https://www.caller.digital/blog/open-source-voice-ai-india-sarvam-ai4bharat-bhasini-2026); [AI4Bharat](https://ai4bharat.iitm.ac.in/); [Bhashini](https://www.bhashini.ai/).
- **Confidence:** medium-high on feasibility; the model-cost engineering is real architectural work, not a free assumption.

### F7: The vernacular-AI tech foundation exists and is state-subsidised — meaningful cost moat.

- AI4Bharat (IIT Madras): IndicTrans2 (22 languages), IndicASR (22 languages), IndicTTS (13+ languages) — Apache 2.0 licensed, free.
- Bhashini: government-backed national language infrastructure, free for non-commercial / discounted commercial. VoicERA open-source voice AI stack on Bhashini announced at India AI Impact Summit 2026.
- TTS MOS quality 3.6–3.9 across major Indian languages — competitive with mid-tier commercial.
- **Implication:** the tech debt-to-build vernacular voice/text AI is materially lower in India in 2026 than it was even 18 months prior. Aura can stand on AI4Bharat + Bhashini and focus its engineering on memory, conversation design, and trust rather than re-building speech infrastructure.
- **Source:** [AI4Bharat](https://ai4bharat.iitm.ac.in/); [Bhashini](https://www.bhashini.ai/); [VoicERA on Bhashini](https://edunovations.com/currentaffairs/national/voicera-ai-stack-on-bhashini/); [Towards TTS for the next billion users (arxiv)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2211.09536).
- **Confidence:** high on availability; medium on quality-at-scale for emotional/conversational use (most published benchmarks are functional, not affective).

## Findings — Moat (mandatory: all 9 types)

For full detail and verdicts, see the table in `docs/foundation/product.md` § Defensibility / Moat. Research basis for each row:

| Moat type | Research basis |
|-----------|----------------|
| Network effects | AI companion apps are 1:1 by nature — no direct network effect. *Indirect* (data network effect from conversational corpus improving model for next users) is real and is folded into "Data / proprietary intelligence." |
| Switching costs | The friend-with-memory thesis. Switching = losing the entity that knows your life story. Wysa's retention (1M+ users sustained) and AI-companion D30 of 13–50% (vs. 5% norm) validate this dynamic empirically. |
| Data / proprietary intelligence | Vernacular emotional/decision conversations at Indian-cultural scale = training corpus no Western LLM has and no Indian competitor currently aggregates. Astrotalk's 35M users generate astrology call data — different domain, not competitive moat. |
| Scale economics | Open-source India-stack (AI4Bharat, Bhashini, Sarvam) means inference cost can be optimised toward floor as Aura scales. Not a *primary* moat — competitors can use the same stack. |
| Brand / trust | In an emotional-counsel category, trust *is* the product. Mass-market Indian users actively distrust algorithmic systems with personal data (well-documented in DPDPA discourse). Aura's stated brand promise (non-judgemental, vernacular, doesn't sell to you) is a deliberate trust play. Brand-building is slow, hard to copy. |
| Regulatory / certification | Wysa shows the bar (FDA Class II, NICE assessment, JMIR-published evidence). Aura is *not* clinical, so this is a credibility floor not a wall — DPDPA compliance + safety guardrails matter; medical certification is out of scope (and out of vision). |
| Distribution / channel | Potential via NGO partnerships, employer assistance programmes, vernacular creator economy. Not a Day-1 moat; an option to deepen. |
| Talent / domain expertise | Indian-language NLP + conversation design + cultural fluency is a rare intersection. Helpful but talent is mobile — not durable. |
| Speed / iteration velocity | Vision explicitly does not bet on out-executing. The classic anti-pattern; correctly avoided. |

**Recommended primary moats to bet on (combine for compounding defence):**
1. **Switching costs** via persistent memory of the user's story.
2. **Data / proprietary intelligence** via vernacular emotional-decision conversation corpus.
3. **Brand / trust** via consistent non-judgemental, fair-priced positioning.

Sources for moat framework: [NFX firm](https://www.nfx.com/), Hamilton Helmer *7 Powers*, public AI-companion retention research above.

## What we couldn't answer

- **Quantified pain in informal-sector Indians specifically** (vision's "office cleaner in Chennai" persona). Most surveys index on formal-sector / student populations. *Recommendation:* commission qualitative interviews (10–20) in first 90 days to validate the vision's stated persona. Logged as Risk R1.
- **Willingness-to-pay at the sub-₹20 price point for *emotional* (not astrological / not utility) AI guidance.** No direct precedent. Astrotalk monetises differently (per-minute). *Recommendation:* test pricing empirically pre-launch.
- **Cultural acceptability of AI as counsel** vs. human (relative, family member, religious figure, astrologer). Acceptability for crisis support (Tele-MANAS) is established; for ongoing decision-companion it is not. Logged as Risk R2.

## Recommendations

1. **Frame the bet as switching-costs + data + brand** moat-wise. These three compound; the others don't pull weight here.
2. **North-star metric should pair scale with depth.** Don't pick MAU alone — Replika and Character.AI both have impressive MAU and significant user-distress concerns. A "Weekly Active Reflector + D30 retention + self-reported clarity score" composite is more honest to the vision.
3. **Out-of-scope must explicitly exclude clinical positioning, crisis-primary mode, English-first, and the astrologer-marketplace model.** All four are tempting adjacent revenue paths that would compromise the vision.
4. **Tech foundation should build on AI4Bharat + Bhashini.** Not strategic differentiation, but a cost-floor that the price point requires.
5. **Commission qualitative work in first 90 days** to validate the informal-sector persona. The desk research above can support the vision's *direction*; only field work can validate the specific persona.
